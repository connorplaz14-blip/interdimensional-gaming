## The TV feed

The initial feed contains three short excerpts from **Universal Modder's documentation teaser**:

- Minecraft × GTA V — 5 seconds.
- Halo × Minecraft — 2 seconds.
- Call of Duty: World at War × Minecraft — 3 seconds.

**Credit:** Rehan Sheikh / rehan-remade and Universal Modder contributors.

[See the original teaser](https://github.com/rehan-remade/universal-modder/blob/0f5dcdfdcd8ed420f8413815bd6647586ab894a2/docs/media/mods-teaser.gif). Its branding is retained. The feed is a 10-second starter loop, not footage of every game in the catalogue.

For anyone checking the details: [source and edit records](research/acquired/PROVENANCE.json) · [included MIT notice](research/acquired/universal-modder-LICENSE.txt). This repository-media notice isn't a blanket licence for unrelated social videos or publisher game assets.

Adding a game to this repo does **not** automatically add its footage to the TV.
