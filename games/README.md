# Choose your next wrong universe

What each project does, who made it, and where to start. **We checked the new additions' documentation and release pages on 6 October 2026; we haven't run or play-tested these mods.** Availability refers to the creator's download, not a compatibility guarantee.

[← Main page](../README.md) · [New to mods? Start here](../START-HERE.md) · [Saved source files](SOURCE-ARCHIVE.md)

## Minecraft × Skyrim

**SkyCraft — chasmlol.** Minecraft movement, inventory, blocks and combat in Skyrim's world.

**Start:** [Creator's guide](https://github.com/chasmlol/SkyCraft#readme) · [Downloads](https://github.com/chasmlol/SkyCraft/releases). Check the matching Skyrim, SKSE and Minecraft requirements. **Source archived here.**

## Minecraft × GTA V

**Universal Modder — Rehan Sheikh / rehan-remade and contributors.** Minecraft building and gameplay in Los Santos, with crossover explosions and interactions.

**Start:** [Setup guide](https://github.com/rehan-remade/universal-modder/tree/main/examples/minecraft-gta5-passthrough). **Setup required; source archived here.** This is an example inside a larger modding toolkit, not a GTA V game download.

## Skate 3 × Modern Warfare 2 × Minecraft

**2010 Rust Rewrite Mashup — chasmlol, building on IW4L by vladtrc and Skate engine work.** Modern Warfare 2, Skate-style skating and a Minecraft world in one project. This covers MW2 × Skate, MW2 × Minecraft and the three-way combination, rather than counting them as unrelated projects.

**Start:** [Creator's guide](https://github.com/chasmlol/2010-rust-rewrite-mashup#readme) · [Downloads](https://github.com/chasmlol/2010-rust-rewrite-mashup/releases). Follow the game-data conversion and setup instructions. **Source archived here.**

## Minecraft × GTA IV

**LibertyCraft — mrborghini and contributors.** Minecraft gameplay in GTA IV's Liberty City.

**Start:** [Creator's guide](https://github.com/mrborghini/libertycraft#readme). **Setup required; source archived here.** Check the supported GTA IV version and Minecraft setup.

## Minecraft × Elden Ring

**Original crossover bridge — justbustin.** Minecraft blocks, movement and inventory in Elden Ring. The original project documents an **Apple Silicon + CrossOver** setup.

**Start:** [Original guide](https://github.com/justbustin/minecraft-crossover-bridge#readme). **Setup required; source archived here.**

**Minecraft Ring — siddoff, based on justbustin's bridge.** A Windows adaptation of the same combination. Minecraft handles the player and blocks while Elden Ring draws the scene; both games stay running.

**Start:** [Windows setup guide](https://github.com/siddoff/Minecraft-Ring#readme). **Experimental, offline single-player; source archived here.** The creator provides source and launch scripts, not a packaged release. This is a separate project, not the original viral clip's creator being credited under another name.

## Minecraft × Monster Hunter: World

**Minecraft crossover bridge — justbustin.** The bridge project also connects Minecraft to Monster Hunter: World.

**Start:** [Creator's guide](https://github.com/justbustin/minecraft-crossover-bridge#readme), then its Monster Hunter section. **Setup required; source archived here.** The documented setup uses Apple Silicon and CrossOver.

## DOOM × Minecraft

**NucleDoom — Patbox and contributors.** DOOM running inside Minecraft, with controls and sound.

**Start:** [Creator's guide](https://github.com/Patbox/nucledoom#readme). **Source archived here.** Our copy excludes DOOM WAD files (game-content files). Follow the original guide for game content and compatible Minecraft versions.

## DOOM × Hytale

**DoomMaps — ssquadteam.** Plays DOOM on Hytale's in-game map display using the VideoMaps infrastructure.

**Start:** [Creator's guide](https://github.com/ssquadteam/DoomMaps#readme). **Setup required; source archived here.** This is DOOM displayed on the world map, not a recreation of its levels as Hytale terrain. Don't confuse it with other DOOM/Hytale projects.

## Minecraft × Valheim

**ValCraft — LoAlCo, based on SkyCraft by chasmlol.** Minecraft blocks, tools and combat in Valheim's terrain, weather and dungeons. Both games run together.

**You need:** Valheim on Steam, Minecraft Java Edition and extra memory for the second game. Follow the creator's PC setup.

**Start:** [Install guide](https://github.com/LoAlCo/ValCraft/blob/main/INSTALL.md) · [Downloads](https://github.com/LoAlCo/ValCraft/releases). **Download available; experimental.** Multiplayer is described as experimental, not fully tested. Files are linked at the original project, not mirrored here.

## Minecraft × Fallout 4

**FalloutCraft — zeyvu, based on SkyCraft by chasmlol.** Build in the Commonwealth and fight Fallout enemies with Minecraft weapons while Fallout keeps its world, quests and Pip-Boy.

**You need:** the supported Steam Fallout 4 version, F4SE, Address Library and Minecraft Java Edition with the matching mod setup. Back up saves; this is an early project.

**Start:** [Install guide](https://github.com/zeyvu/FalloutCraft#installing) · [Downloads](https://github.com/zeyvu/FalloutCraft/releases). **Download available; experimental.** Original-project files only.

## Minecraft × Outer Wilds

**OWCraft — Yaekai, based on SkyCraft by chasmlol.** Fly to a planet, step out and switch into Minecraft to build there. The creator says builds persist across time loops and restarts.

**You need:** Windows, Outer Wilds on Steam, its Mod Manager, and Minecraft Java Edition with the required Fabric setup.

**Start:** [Install guide](https://github.com/Yaekai/OWCraft#readme) · [Downloads](https://github.com/Yaekai/OWCraft/releases). **Download available; experimental; source archived here.** The creator reports testing on one Windows PC; broader compatibility isn't established.

## Minecraft × ULTRAKILL

**Killcraft — goonsn / the Killcraft authors, based on SkyCraft by chasmlol.** Fight through ULTRAKILL levels with Minecraft movement, weapons, health, blocks and TNT.

**You need:** ULTRAKILL on Steam, BepInEx 5 and an account that owns Minecraft Java Edition. The package sets up its Minecraft launcher on first use.

**Start:** [Install guide](https://github.com/goonsn/Killcraft#install) · [Downloads](https://github.com/goonsn/Killcraft/releases). **Download available; experimental; source archived here.** Native ULTRAKILL weapons and movement aren't active while Minecraft controls the player.

## Skate 3 × Garry's Mod

**SkateGM — the-schwilliam.** Skate 3 physics, tricks and scoring on Garry's Mod maps, with multiplayer, minigames and a park editor.

**You need:** Windows, Garry's Mod's x86-64 branch and your own Xbox 360 Skate 3 files. A controller is recommended.

**Start:** [Install guide](https://github.com/the-schwilliam/SkateGM#install) · [Downloads](https://github.com/the-schwilliam/SkateGM/releases). **Download available.** Original-project files only; follow its conversion instructions.

## Mario 64 × Elden Ring

**ER Mario — Deltaroo / deltarooo, using libsm64.** Mario's real jumps, wall kicks and attacks meet Elden Ring's world and enemies. Not just a Mario costume.

**You need:** Elden Ring on Steam, the me3 mod loader and your own US-version Super Mario 64 ROM.

**Start:** [Getting started](https://github.com/deltarooo/er-mario#getting-started) · [Downloads](https://github.com/deltarooo/er-mario/releases). **Download available; work in progress; offline only.** The creator explicitly says never to play it online. Original-project files only.

## Skate 3 × Bully

**BullySkate — Faiqie, using SK8-ENGINE's Skate engine work.** Ride around Bullworth as Jimmy with Skate 3 physics.

**You need:** Windows 10/11, Bully Scholarship Edition version 1.200 and your own complete Xbox 360 Skate 3 files.

**Start:** [Setup guide](https://github.com/Faiqie/BullySkate#setup) · [Downloads](https://github.com/Faiqie/BullySkate/releases). **Download available.** Original-project files only.

## Skate 3 × GTA San Andreas

**GTA San AnSkateas — ryglizzy, using Skate engine and 2010 Mashup work.** CJ skates San Andreas' streets with Skate-style controls, tricks, grinds and bails.

**You need:** classic PC GTA San Andreas (1.0 US, **not** Definitive Edition), an ASI loader, your own Xbox 360 Skate 3 files and a controller.

**Start:** [Install guide](https://github.com/ryglizzy/GTA-San-AnSkateas#download-and-install) · [Downloads](https://github.com/ryglizzy/GTA-San-AnSkateas/releases). **Download available.** Original-project files only; use the creator's preparation steps.

## Minecraft × Subnautica

**SubCraft — FumperForrest, with architecture derived from SkyCraft.** Connects Minecraft's player, blocks and mobs to Subnautica's underwater world.

**Start:** [Follow development](https://github.com/FumperForrest/SubCraft#readme). **Early development, not a finished player download.** Its README describes completed prototypes and collision work in progress. Source is linked upstream, not archived here.
