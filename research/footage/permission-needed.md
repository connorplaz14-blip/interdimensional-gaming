## Permission scope to request

Ask for written permission covering all of these, not just “can we use the clip?”:

- edit short excerpts and longer sequences;
- crop/scale to 16:9 and colour-normalize;
- remove/mute original audio if desired;
- loop the footage inside a CRT-styled web player;
- self-host the resulting MP4/WebM on the INTER DIMENSIONAL GAMING website/CDN;
- include the footage in the project’s continuous montage;
- credit the creator/project and link the original source;
- retain the permission record in the project metadata.

Do **not** treat a like, repost, public upload, source-code licence, or platform embed permission as permission to download/edit/rehost the video.
